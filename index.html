Before IT Era vs Now IT Era

Before IT Era

  • Wrote with pen/typewriter and when you an errors it meant rewriting the whole page.
  • Stored all records in physical folders, cabinets, and boxes.
  • Sent letters by postoffice - took days or weeks to arrive.
  • Researched only inside libraries using printed books.
  • Held classes only face-to-face and no online materials.
  • Shopped only by visiting physical stores personally.

Now IT Era

  • Type and edit files instantly and you can save unlimited versions.
  • Store files on USB or cloud accessible anywhere, anytime.
  • Chat, call, or send files globally in seconds.
  • Find any information online in just one search or click.
  • Attend classes, submit work, and study fully online.
  • Order anything online and have it delivered at home.
